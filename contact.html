BEFORE WE BEGIN

A clearer path
to working together.

Every engagement starts with understanding your context. Here are a few things to know before your first conversation with our team.

Where should we start?

Tell us about the business challenge, the people involved, and the outcome you want. A first conversation helps us identify whether you need advisory support, capacity development, or a combination of both.

Can you tailor support to our organisation?

Our services include custom corporate training, executive coaching, and business advisory. We discuss your sector, operating environment, and priorities before agreeing the scope of an engagement.

Do you work with international organisations?

SSCI serves local and international markets. Our Nigerian context is especially relevant to organisations navigating energy operations, local content requirements, workforce development, and stakeholder relationships.

How is progress assessed?

We agree objectives and relevant performance measures at the start. These provide a basis for reviewing implementation and discussing the changes that matter to your organisation.
