SKILLS AND SUCCESS CONSULTING INTERNATIONAL

From potential
to lasting performance.

SSCI is a management and energy consulting firm helping organisations connect people development with business performance.

Founded in 2023, our company grew from a passion for helping individuals and organisations recognise their gaps to potential and develop a practical strategy to close them.

We bring together five areas of expertise: management and business advisory; energy, oil and gas consulting; capacity building; project management and social impact; and digital transformation.

Our work focuses on eliminating waste, improving effectiveness, and helping teams become more agile. We combine an international perspective with understanding of Nigeria’s business environment, particularly the Niger Delta energy corridor.

From strategy and organisational design to workforce development and implementation, we help leaders connect their objectives with the skills and systems needed to deliver.
